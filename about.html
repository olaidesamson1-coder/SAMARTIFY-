About SamArtify

I’m a Nigerian entrepreneur building SamArtify to bring professional embroidery designs to makers everywhere. Rooted in a deep love for textiles and craft, my goal is to make beautiful, unique designs accessible to hobbyists and businesses globally.

Our Story

Growing up in Nigeria, I was surrounded by rich textile traditions and bold patterns. My journey into digital embroidery began when I combined traditional motifs with modern machine-ready formats. What started as a passion project quickly became SamArtify — a platform to share curated, high-quality embroidery files with the world.

Our Mission

We exist to expand global access to exceptional embroidery patterns. Our mission is to remove barriers for creators everywhere by offering easy-to-use, professionally digitized designs, clear format support, and trustworthy customer service.

Unique Designs

Every design blends cultural inspiration with precise digitizing techniques so your finished work looks crisp and professional. We focus on unique motifs, clean stitch paths, and multi-format delivery for popular embroidery machines.
