Digital Delivery

How will I receive my design files after purchase? ▼
After completing your purchase, you'll receive an instant download link via email. Your files are also available in your account dashboard under "My Downloads." You can access and download your files anytime within 30 days of purchase. No physical items will be shipped.
What file formats will I receive? ▼
We provide embroidery design files in multiple formats including .PES, .JEF, .EXP, .VIP, and .ART. Each design package includes all compatible formats so you can use them with virtually any embroidery machine. High-resolution PDF preview files are also included.
How soon will I receive my download link? ▼
Download links are generated instantly upon successful payment. You'll receive an automated email within 5 minutes with your download link and access credentials. If you don't see it, please check your spam folder or contact our support team.
Can I re-download my files if I lose them? ▼
Yes! You can download your files anytime from your account dashboard. Simply log in, go to "My Downloads," and click the download button for any design you've purchased. Your files are stored securely for 30 days after purchase.
Is there a data size limit for files? ▼
Most design files are between 1-10MB. If you experience slow downloads, we recommend checking your internet connection or trying a different browser. Contact support if you experience issues larger than 5 minutes to download a complete package.

Can I share the design files with others? ▼

Machine Compatibility

Which embroidery machines are compatible with these files? ▼
Our designs work with virtually all modern embroidery machines. We provide files in formats compatible with machines from Brother, Janome, Bernina, Husqvarna, Singer, Pfaff, Babylock, and many others. Check your machine manual for supported file formats.
What if my machine doesn't recognize the file format? ▼
We provide multiple file formats in each package. Try a different format included in your download. You can also use free conversion software like Inkscape or paid options like Wilcom Truesizer. If you're still having trouble, contact our support team with your machine model.
What's the maximum design size for different machines? ▼
Design sizes vary by machine hooping area. Our designs range from 2" x 2" to 6" x 6" to accommodate most machines. Check your machine's maximum embroidery field (e.g., 4" x 4", 5" x 7", 8" x 8"). All designs can be resized smaller, but enlarging may reduce stitch quality.
Can I resize the designs? ▼
Yes, you can resize designs using your embroidery machine software. We recommend reducing size rather than enlarging to maintain stitch quality. Our designs are optimized for 100% scale; resizing beyond 120% may affect quality. Most designs scale well down to 50%.
What embroidery software do I need? ▼
Most modern embroidery machines include free software (like Brother PE-Design, Janome Digitizer) that can load our files directly. For editing, you can use free options (Inkscape) or premium software (Wilcom, Bernina Software). Your machine's software is usually sufficient for just stitching our designs.
Are there stitch count requirements? ▼
Most machines support designs with 20,000+ stitches. Our designs typically range from 5,000-50,000 stitches. Check your machine manual for maximum stitch capacity. All file specifications are provided in the product details to help you decide before purchase.
