🔴 Critical Refund Rules

⚠️ No Refunds for Accessed Files

Once you download and access your design files, they cannot be refunded. This policy is final and non-negotiable. By clicking "Download," you acknowledge that you are accepting the file in its current state.

  • ❌ No Refunds After Download

    Design files are digital products. Once downloaded, they cannot be returned. You bear responsibility for verifying file compatibility before download.

  • ❌ No Refunds for Change of Mind

    If you change your mind about a design after preview, we cannot offer a refund. Take time to review the preview images and specifications before purchasing.

  • ❌ No Refunds for Shared Files

    If files have been shared, transferred, or distributed to others, refunds cannot be issued. Each purchase is licensed to the individual buyer only.

  • ❌ No Refunds for Commercial Use Misuse

    If you purchased with Personal Use license but used the files commercially, refunds will not be issued. Ensure you purchase the correct license type.

  • ✓ Preview Before Purchase

    We provide detailed previews, specifications, stitch counts, file formats, and customer reviews. Please review all information carefully before purchasing.

✅ When Refunds ARE Allowed

Refunds MAY be issued in the following situations:

  • Technical Issues (Before Download)

    If the file fails to download, corrupts, or cannot be accessed within 24 hours of purchase, refunds can be issued if reported immediately.

  • File Format Error

    If advertised file formats are missing or incorrect (e.g., promised .PES file not included), you may request a refund within 7 days of discovery.

  • Design Quality Defect

    If the design contains obvious stitching errors, distortions, or quality issues that differ significantly from the preview, refunds may be issued within 7 days of purchase.

  • System Error/Duplicate Charge

    If you were charged twice for the same purchase or experienced a system error, we will issue a full refund immediately.

  • Design Removed/Unavailable

    If a design is removed from our catalog due to copyright violations or policy violations after your purchase, a full refund will be issued.

Important: To qualify for a refund in these situations, you must contact support@samartify.com within 7 days of purchase with proof (screenshots, error messages, or detailed description).

📋 How to Request a Refund

1

Submit Your Request

Email support@samartify.com with your order number, reason for refund, and any supporting evidence (screenshots, error messages, etc.). Subject: "REFUND REQUEST - [Order #]"

2

We Review Your Case

Our support team reviews your request within 24-48 business hours. We may ask clarifying questions or request additional documentation to verify your claim.

3

Decision & Approval

We notify you via email whether your refund has been approved or denied. If approved, we process the refund immediately. If denied, we explain why with reference to this policy.

4

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days. Your bank may take an additional 2-3 business days to show the credit.

5

Confirmation

You receive a confirmation email once the refund has been processed. Check your bank account within 7-10 days total from approval.

Not eligible for refund? We may offer store credit instead. Store credit can be used for future purchases and is valid for 12 months from issuance. This is at our discretion and only if you request it within the 7-day window.
  • Store Credit vs. Refund

    If you prefer store credit instead of a refund, we may approve this for purchases that don't qualify for cash refunds. Request this when contacting support.

  • Credit Expiration

    Store credits expire 12 months from issue date. If unused after 12 months, the credit is forfeited with no refund or extension available.

  • Non-Transferable

    Store credits are tied to your account and cannot be transferred, sold, or shared with other users.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund if I don't like the design after purchasing?
No. Once you purchase, you have reviewed all available previews, specifications, and reviews. If you change your mind after purchasing, no refund will be issued. This is why we provide extensive preview information.
What happens if I download the file then want a refund?
Once downloaded, files cannot be refunded under any circumstances. Downloads are final. Do not download the file if you're unsure about the design.
Can I return a file within 30 days instead of 7?
No. The refund window is strictly 7 days from purchase for most issues. Technical issues may be eligible for refund within 30 days if reported immediately, but downloaded files cannot be refunded.
How long do refunds take to appear in my bank account?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days, but your bank may take an additional 2-3 business days to show the credit. Total time is typically 10 days. International transfers may take longer.
What if I don't receive my refund within 10 days?
Contact your bank first to check if the refund is pending. If not found, contact us at support@samartify.com with your order number and refund confirmation email. We'll investigate.
Can I resell a design if I'm not happy with it?
No. Design files cannot be resold, redistributed, or shared. Attempting to do so violates our license agreement and may result in account termination.
What if there's a billing error and I was charged twice?
Contact us immediately at support@samartify.com. System errors are processed as emergency refunds and can be issued same-day with proof of duplicate charge.
Can I get a refund in another form (gift card, store credit)?
Yes. Instead of a cash refund, we may offer store credit (valid 12 months) for eligible claims. Request this when submitting your refund request.

Have Questions About Our Refund Policy?

Our support team is here to help. Contact us within 7 days of purchase for any refund-related questions.
