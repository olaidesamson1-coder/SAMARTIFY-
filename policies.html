Refund Policy

Eligibility for Refunds

SamArtify offers refunds for digital design files within 7 days of purchase under the following circumstances:

  • Technical Issues: File corruption, missing formats, or inability to open the design files
  • Description Mismatch: The design significantly differs from the product preview or description
  • Quality Issues: Stitch errors, distorted designs, or quality defects not visible in preview
  • File Format Problems: Provided formats are incompatible with advertised machine types
Non-Refundable Items

Refunds cannot be issued for:

  • Change of mind or buyer's remorse after reviewing files
  • Personal preference or style changes
  • Designs already transferred to your embroidery machine
  • Downloads made more than 7 days after purchase
  • Products purchased during sales or with promotional discounts (excluding defective items)
  • User error in converting or modifying files
Refund Process

Step 1: Contact our support team at support@samartify.com within 7 days of purchase with your order number

Step 2: Provide a detailed description of the issue, including screenshots or error messages if applicable

Step 3: Our team reviews your claim within 24 business hours

Step 4: If approved, refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days

Step 5: You will receive an email confirmation once the refund is complete

⚠️ Important: Bank processing times may add 2-3 business days. Your bank may show the refund as "pending" for up to 10 days before it appears in your account.
No Refund for Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same design twice, we can provide a store credit for one purchase instead of a refund. Store credits are valid for 12 months and can be applied to any future purchase on SamArtify.

Exceptional Circumstances

SamArtify reserves the right to issue refunds for exceptional circumstances outside the standard policy, including system errors, billing disputes, or at our sole discretion. Contact support@samartify.com with details for consideration.
